Step 8 of 8

What PASS, WARNING and FAIL mean

MultiCamApp
PASS

Ready to use

Every frame the camera delivered was saved, nothing was duplicated or padded, and the timing records match the video.

What to do: nothing. The video is ready for analysis.
WARNING

Usually usable, needs a look

The frames are intact, but the timing was uneven or some details are incomplete. The result explains why.

What to do: read the note. For timing-sensitive work, use the timestamp file.
FAIL

Do not use it for analysis yet

Frames were lost or duplicated, a file is missing or damaged, or the timing record does not match the video.

What to do: check the reason shown, fix the cause, and record again if needed.

A folder's overall result is the worst result among its videos, so one FAIL makes the whole folder FAIL.
